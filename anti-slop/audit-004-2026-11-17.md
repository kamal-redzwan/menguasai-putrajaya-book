# Report 004: taste-skill redesign (2026-11-17)

**Mode:** AFTER, session choice. After the campaign-poster version the owner said it still read as generic
AI slop and asked for a redesign with taste-skill (github.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill), with no fetched photographs
and the designer's own generated images instead.
**Branch:** `staging-claude`.

## What changed
- The whole site now runs on taste-skill 2.5.1's kit (`public/assets`, copied unedited): one system for grid,
  type, buttons, nav, footer, forms and motion. Tailwind and the earlier hand-written layout are removed.
- Every structural choice was dealt by the skill's scripts rather than picked by taste (see `DESIGN.md`).
- **Pictures:** a generated Dewan Rakyat seat chart (hero), the client's cover files, a generated cover pair,
  and frames cut from the client's two videos. Nothing was fetched from the web.
- The CHIP Collect checkout keeps its request and payload unchanged. The form now uses the kit's validation
  (errors under each field, in Malay), and a failed payment redirect shows a message.
- The videos play only when the visitor presses play (they no longer autoplay on scroll).

## taste-skill finish gate
`GATE: OPEN 1 (rounds used up)`. The one open item was a phone-width gap after the order section. It was fixed
after the third round: a checker run afterwards at 1440 and 390 no longer lists it.

Waivers (`.taste-skill/waivers.json`, 3 of 5 allowed):
1. `phone-number`: CHIP card-payment activation needs a reachable business phone; the owner supplied it.
2. `postcode`: CHIP needs the registered business address in full.
3. `product-shown`: no photographs by request, so the hero is the generated seat chart; the book is shown whole
   in the book section.

Still flagged by the stricter, non-gate rhythm walk at tablet and phone widths: sections do not land on exact
whole-screen multiples (`section-height`, `one-section`). They read fine in screenshots; this checker detail
was left.
