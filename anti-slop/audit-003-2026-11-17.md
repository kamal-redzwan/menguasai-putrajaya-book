# Antislop Audit Report 003 (2026-11-17)

**Mode:** AFTER (full-site visual audit, requested as a "whole site redesign")
**Scope:** `src/pages/index.astro`, `src/pages/berjaya.astro`, the four policy pages, every file in `src/components/`, `DESIGN.md`, `tailwind.config.mjs`
**Method:** `astro build`, then full-page screenshots of `/`, `/polisi-privasi/` and `/berjaya/` at 1440px and 390px, plus source reading.
**Skills applied:** `antislop` (core), `antislop-ui`, `antislop-layoutmobile`, `antislop-code`

Audits 001 and 002 checked rules one at a time and found nothing open. This audit looks at the page as a whole. Most of the problems come from the same few patterns repeated on every section, so no single rule flags them.

Priority follows the rule tier: Hard Gate = HIGH, Purpose-Gate = MEDIUM, Quality Lock = LOW. Several LOW items have a large visual effect; the tier sets the label, not the size of the problem.

---

## HIGH (Hard Gate)

**1. Quota bar shows a made-up stock level and leaks a placeholder to customers** (R-17, R-38)
`CheckoutCTA.astro:38-56`. The bar is hard-coded to `w-3/4` (75% sold), and `aria-valuenow={totalQuota}` tells screen readers it is 100% full. Neither number comes from real order data. The visible label reads `Kuota: 200 Naskhah Terawal ([DATA SEBENAR] Stok Langsung)`, so the `[DATA SEBENAR]` marker meant for the developer appears on the live checkout. Fix: remove the bar and say "Terhad kepada 200 naskhah cetakan pertama". Bring the bar back only when it can read a real order count.

## MEDIUM (Purpose-Gate)

**2. Too much yellow: the accent appears 113 times** (R-01, Part 3 "One deliberate accent", Part 1 "Excessive Accent Color")
Yellow is used on every eyebrow, a headline word, both prices, the quota text, "PRU 16" in body prose, the spec numbers, the SSM number in the footer heading, the phone and email links, the required-field asterisks, the radio buttons and every focus ring. Because it is everywhere, it no longer marks anything, including the buy button. Fix: keep yellow for the buy action, the selected price and focus rings. Make everything else white or muted text.

**3. Small uppercase yellow label above every heading** (R-06, Part 1 "Eyebrow Badge" and "Generic AI Typography")
The same `text-xs font-bold uppercase tracking-widest text-yellow-400` label sits above every heading: "Pengenalan Visual", "Sinopsis Kulit Belakang", "Fakta & Metodologi Karya", "Tinjauan Strategik", "Pra-Jualan Cetakan Pertama", "Maklumat Syarikat & Hubungan Rasmi", "Dokumen & Polisi Rasmi", "Dokumen Rasmi • Privacy Policy" and "Pengesahan Pembayaran CHIP Collect". There are 31 uppercase-tracked strings across 10 files. These labels make every section read as the same template. Fix: remove most of them and keep one only where it adds information the heading does not.

**4. Bordered rounded boxes nested inside other boxes** (R-14, Part 1 "Sterile Default")
Almost every block of content sits in a `rounded-xl/2xl border border-slate-700 bg-slate-900` box. Some examples: the checkout box holds a quota box and a summary box; the synopsis ends with a publication-details box; the specs use two cards plus a bordered strip; the footer has an address box and four stacked link cards; the policy pages put a contact box inside a document box. The result is grey outlines on navy with no depth or texture, which is the "safe default" look. Fix: use spacing, rules and type size to separate content. Keep a box only for the order form.

**5. Typeface does not match the book** (R-06, R-20)
The cover uses a tall condensed display face for the title. The site uses Inter at weights 400 to 900 for everything, and `DESIGN.md` gives a legibility reason that would fit any website. The site therefore shares no type with the book it sells. Fix: a condensed display face for headings, close to the cover's (for example Oswald or Barlow Condensed), and a serif with good Malay diacritic support for long prose (for example Source Serif 4 or Literata). Inter can stay for the form and data.

## LOW (Quality Locks)

**6. Hero shows the title twice side by side** (R-20, R-05)
The cover image already shows "SIAPA AKAN MENGUASAI PUTRAJAYA?" in large type, and the H1 repeats it right next to it. The phrase "Sebuah Analisis Berasaskan Lensa Politik Pasca-Normal" appears three times: on the cover, in the hero label and in the synopsis subheading. On a 390px screen the cover fills the first screen, so the title and buy button only appear around 1,100px down. Fix: make the cover the main element on desktop. On mobile, show the title, price and button first, then the cover.

**7. Prices and SSM number repeated too often** (R-05 / C-3 content-driven composition)
Prices appear 5 times on the home page (hero, the "200" spec card, checkout intro, quota label, zone cards). "BRAINWAVE COMMUNICATIONS (202203208117 / JM0967023-W)" appears 16 times across the source, up to 4 times on one screen (checkout footnote, footer heading, address block, copyright). Fix: show prices once near the top and once at checkout. Show the SSM number once in the footer and once in the payment note.

**8. Specs section heading does not match its content** (R-05, R-14)
`SpecsGrid.astro`: the heading says "Empat Tunjang Utama Buku Ini" (four pillars of the book), but two of the four items are the page count and the print run, and "430" sits directly above "430 Muka Surat". Fix: turn page count, ISBN, publisher and edition into a compact book-details list, and show the two method items as short prose or a quote.

**9. Video sections leave large empty areas** (R-05, RHYTHM dial)
Video 01 is a 384px-wide portrait video centred in a 1440px page, with empty space on both sides. In video 02 the text column is vertically centred beside a tall video, which leaves about 250px of empty space above the text. `DESIGN.md` sets RHYTHM to 3 (varied), but sections only differ by background colour and centred-versus-split layout (Part 1 "Alternating Background Only"). Fix: put the two videos side by side as one "Tonton" section, with the copy beside or above them.

**10. Video labels repeat what the controls show** (R-05 copy, R-39)
"KLIK VIDEO UNTUK AUDIO" sits above each video while the "Suara Dimatikan" button below says the same thing, and clicking the video itself toggles sound without a visible cue. Fix: remove the label and keep the control buttons.

**11. Mixed corner radius and button casing** (R-11, R-20)
Corners use `rounded-lg`, `rounded-xl` and `rounded-2xl` with no clear rule. The buy button is uppercase and tracked in the header but sentence case in the hero. The hero labels "(TERMASUK POS)" are set at 10px. Fix: two radii (small for controls, none or large for media), one button style, and no text below 12px.

**12. Policy pages repeat the footer** (R-05)
Each policy page ends with a "Maklumat Syarikat" contact box, and the footer directly below shows the same address, phone and email. The four document links appear as a tab row at the top and again as four cards in the footer. Fix: keep the tabs and the footer, and remove the in-page contact box.

**13. Video files are heavy and have no poster** (R-40, mechanism only, not measured)
The two WhatsApp exports are 8.6MB and 8.1MB, with spaces in the filenames, `preload="metadata"` and no `poster`, so each frame is black until data arrives. Fix: add a poster frame for each video. Re-encoding the files to a smaller size is optional.

## Proposed direction for the redesign (R-37, needs owner approval)

Reading this as: a single-title political book sales page for Malaysian readers, in a newspaper front-page / campaign-poster style taken from the cover, dial `ENERGY 3 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 1`.

- **Palette:** keep navy `#09111e` and yellow `#FACC15` from the cover. Add one off-white paper tone (about `#F4F1EA`) for the synopsis and order sections, so the page alternates between night-time cover and printed page. Yellow is used only for the buy action.
- **Type:** condensed display headings (like the cover), a serif for the synopsis, and Inter only for the form and numbers.
- **Repeated motif:** thin rules and column dividers like a newspaper front page, with section numbers styled as chapter markers. These replace the boxes and the small labels.
- **Motion:** hover states only, plus the existing play-on-scroll videos. MOTION is lowered from 2 to 1 because the videos already provide the movement.
