# Audit 006: the standard Book Page (2026-11-23)

**Mode:** AFTER, session choice.
**Branch:** `claude/clever-fermat-jyp7fi`.
**Scope:** the new `src/pages/[category]/[book].astro` and what it needed in `OrderSection.astro`, `KitLayout.astro`,
`catalog.ts` and `api/checkout.ts`.

## What was built
Every Book the owner publishes in the EmDash admin now has a page at `/<category>/<book>`, built from its fields
(cover, title, author, Status, the two prices, ISBN, page count, short synopsis). It follows the site's taste-skill
deal in `DESIGN.md`: the owner's cover-left hero on the jacket's navy, `word-highlight` on the synopsis, the order
form, the rest of the Category's shelf (up to four Books, the same card as the Category page) and the buyer's
questions answered with this Book's prices.

- A Book that is not published, or is not in the Category in the address, is a 404.
- Pre-order: the button reads "Pra-tempah buku" and the delivery lines say the Book is posted once printed.
- Akan datang (or no prices yet): no order form and no FAQ; the hero says the price is to be announced and links
  to the Category; a "Butiran buku" section lists what is known.
- The hand-built page for *Siapa Akan Menguasai Putrajaya?* stays; Astro serves it before the dynamic route.
- Checkout: the form sends the Book's slug, and `/api/checkout` reads the title and price from EmDash, so the
  CHIP receipt names the right Book and a reader cannot change the price in the browser. A Book that is not
  orderable is refused with a 400.

Checked in a local dev server with three test Books (On sale with every field, Pre-order with no ISBN, page
count or synopsis, Akan datang with no prices) at 1440 and 390: every page renders, nothing scrolls sideways, and
there are no console errors. A missing Book and a Book under the wrong Category return 404. The checkout refused
a missing slug and an Akan datang Book, and let an On sale Book through to the CHIP step. The Putrajaya order form
is unchanged.

Not run: taste-skill's finish gate (`gate.mjs`) and `consistency.mjs`. They are not installed in this project,
and the page needs the EmDash database, so it has no static file to check.

## Findings

| # | Priority | Rule | Finding |
|---|----------|------|---------|
| 1 | MEDIUM | R-05 | With a short synopsis, the `word-highlight` section is two screens tall but its text fills about a third of the first. The rest is empty paper (see the 1440 screenshot, Akan datang Book). The Putrajaya page has the same moment, but with a longer statement. Fix: give the statement section `--h: 1` when the synopsis is under about 25 words, or show it as plain text without the moment. |
| 2 | MEDIUM | R-05 / R-38 | An Akan datang Book with no ISBN or page count gets a "Butiran buku" section with only two rows (author, Category), which the hero already shows. That makes a mostly empty screen that repeats facts. Fix: drop the section when it would repeat the hero, and keep its "belum boleh ditempah" line in the hero. |
| 3 | MEDIUM | R-23 | A Book has one picture, its cover. taste-skill's product plan wants a different picture in each section, so the cover leads the hero only and the order section's side holds the Book's details. A back-cover or spread image would give the order section a real picture. Fix: add an optional "Back cover" image field to the Books collection in EmDash, and show it beside the order form when present. This needs the owner's go-ahead, because it changes the admin. |
| 4 | LOW | R-31 | The hero's third fact is the page count when there is one, otherwise the Status. It sits in the same row as the two prices, so "210" reads like a price at a glance. Fix: label it "210 muka surat" in the value, or move it under the author line. |
| 5 | LOW | R-24 | The "Buku" menu and the footer's "Buku terbaru" column still name only *Siapa Akan Menguasai Putrajaya?* on every page, the new Book Pages included. Fix: read the newest Featured Book from EmDash for both. |

Pass: the cover image is not lazy, has `fetchpriority="high"`, and uses the admin's width and height (R-40). Every
cover has alt text, falling back to "Kulit hadapan buku <title> karya <author>" (R-39). Prices come only from the
admin, and nothing is invented: a Book with no prices says "akan diumumkan" (R-36, R-38). Buttons are a verb and
an object: Tempah buku, Pra-tempah buku, Baca sinopsis buku, Lihat buku <Category> (R-15).
