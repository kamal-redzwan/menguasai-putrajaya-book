# Brainwave Communications book storefront

The online shop of Brainwave Communications, where readers browse its books by category and order a printed
copy of one book at a time.

## Language

### The catalogue

**Storefront**:
The whole public site: the homepage and every page a reader can browse or order from.
_Avoid_: Shop, store page, website

**Book**:
One title Brainwave Communications sells, with its cover, price and details. *Siapa Akan Menguasai
Putrajaya?* is the first.
_Avoid_: Product, item, title

**Category**:
A shelf a Book belongs to, such as Umum Politik. The six are Umum Politik, Umum Motivasi, Buku Novel Remaja,
Buku Novel Kanak-kanak, Buku Tokoh-tokoh and Buku Ilmiah.
_Avoid_: Genre, collection, tag

**Upcoming Category**:
A Category with no Book that is Pre-order or On sale, shown to readers as "Akan datang".
_Avoid_: Empty category, coming soon page

**Availability**:
Whether a Book can be ordered: Pre-order (ordered now, sent after printing), On sale (in stock), or Akan datang
(announced, not yet orderable).
_Avoid_: Status (that word means draft or published in the CMS), stock

**Featured Book**:
A Book the owner has chosen to show on the homepage.
_Avoid_: Highlight, pick, bestseller

**Book Page**:
The page for one Book, where a reader learns about it and places an Order.
_Avoid_: Landing page, product page

### Buying

**Order**:
A reader's paid request for one or more copies of a single Book, delivered to one address.
_Avoid_: Purchase, cart, checkout

### Proof

**Testimonial**:
A real reader's words about a Book, shown with their name. Never written by us.
_Avoid_: Review, quote
